# Desktop Companion

Fox lives only in the World window (owner request 2026-10-29: Fox no longer stays on the desktop). There is no floating Companion window: closing the World window hides it while the app and its background work keep running, and minimizing or another app coming to the foreground leaves Fox where it is. The Dock icon (Mac), the notification-area (tray) icon (Windows) or launching Worldlet again brings the World back; Linux adds no icon. Websites and the inline player stop when the World hides; Fox's own task page keeps working. Quitting Worldlet still stops the store and Agent runtime normally.

- One message bar sits under Fox (owner decision 2026-10-04, like Claude's composer): the field, then the microphone and Send inside it. The bar has two sizes (owner Order 2026-10-08): 232px at rest (it also takes a web address in a website) and 300px when pointed at or typing. It is the same frosted glass as the buttons beside Fox, so it blends with the scene without hiding it, and always says “Message <Agent>” (owner Order 2026-10-12); only voice and Talk say what they are doing. Pointing at the bar or Fox opens it to its typing width (owner request 2026-10-12) without starting to type. Once voice starts (the microphone, a hold, Talk or an Order) the bar keeps the length it had until the voice ends (owner Order 2026-10-12). The open bar looks like Claude's (owner requests 2026-10-12) in the theme's colours: “+” at its far left adds files the person picks to the line as paths, the bug after it when Orders can be sent, then the line, the microphone and Send, dimmed until there is something to send. "+", the bug, the microphone, ✕ and Stop are one kind of button: a plain icon on the bar's glass with a light frost under the pointer (owner Order 2026-10-12). A click on the microphone records with ✕ at the left, a dotted sound wave, Stop (the words go into the line to edit) and Send; and Send shows only once there is something to send; a click starts typing and opens it to 360px, a stationary 280ms hold on it speaks instead. Clicking Fox does the same as clicking the bar (owner feedback 2026-10-04): it starts typing. The buttons beside Fox share one style, a frosted capsule with an icon and its name, at most three a side, stacked top to bottom and centered on Fox (owner requests 2026-10-05); on the left, Settings, then Order under it in Alpha and Dev. **Settings** opens the centered Companion Panel straight on its Settings tab (owner Order 2026-10-07; the tutorial's on/off switch is in the World's top-right corner); hold Fox to speak and release to finish. Send is an icon the same size and style as the microphone. The microphone supports click-to-record and hold-to-speak. Thinking displays three dots above Fox; active work and transcription display a short status there. Reduced motion keeps the dots static. The same built-in Fox artwork and reply paper are used.
- Closing the window leaves background work running. Right-click Fox (or the Dock icon on Mac, the tray icon on Windows) for Back to World and Quit Completely. The app menu labels Command/Ctrl-Q as Quit Completely; explicit termination still shuts down the runtime normally. On Windows, right-clicking the taskbar button also offers Quit Worldlet (a Jump List task that launches the app with `--quit`; the running copy quits), since that menu's Close window only closes the World. Quitting hides Fox and the World at once, gives quit work four seconds, then ends every process the app spawned that is still running, with its children, before exiting ([`src/host/quit.ts`](../host/quit.ts); owner report 2026-10-04: Fox stayed on the desktop after quitting). The Windows update installer is the exception: it waits for that quit to replace the app (`outliveQuit`).
- Until onboarding is over (setup, then the first-run tour and first task, `onboardingUnfinished` in `agent/companion`), closing the World window quits Worldlet and minimizing only minimizes (owner request 2026-10-04). The next launch resumes setup or the tour where the person left it.
- Run in background ([web](../web/README.md#web)): closing hides every Worldlet window and gives the foreground back (Mac), and a login launch opens none.

Before 2026-10-29 closing, minimizing or switching apps moved the live World view into a transparent always-on-top window around Fox; that is in Git history. The page still answers the old `desktopCompanion*` actions, which the host now treats as no-ops (`desktopCompanionState` is always off).

## Ownership

[`host/modules/companion/window.ts`](../host/modules/companion/window.ts) owns hiding and restoring the World window; [`host/modules/app/index.ts`](../host/modules/app/index.ts) registers its page actions and the Back to World / Quit Completely menus, [`host/modules/companion/tray.ts`](../host/modules/companion/tray.ts) owns that shared menu and the Windows tray icon, and the World window's close hook asks it whether to keep the app open.
