# Tasks

Work that runs without the person: routines and cron jobs, source checks, background drafts, Applet tasks and the work brought in from the person's Agent. Fox starts some tasks and Applets start others; the person's Agent does the work, and each result becomes an [artifact](../artifacts/README.md). See the [feature table](../../docs/UI-CORE-PLATFORM.md#features).

- Scheduling (`agent-work.ts`, `background-work.ts`, `runtime-*.ts`, `routine-catch-up.ts`, `source-checks.ts`, `deliveries.ts`, `operations.ts`): when work is due, who may run it, claims, leases and retries.
- Ongoing things (`ongoing.ts`, `kinds.ts`, `themes.ts`, `attention.ts`, `external-events.ts`, `harness-calls.ts`): conversations, outside events and calls that keep going over days.

## Ongoing things

An **ongoing thing** is a conversation brought from another Agent that is really one job carried on over days, made into its own Applet (owner design 2026-10-03, the [mobile Applet world](../phone/README.md#payloads)). An OpenClaw channel such as "Discord · #diet-and-health" or a long Claude Code session such as "worldlet · Sign-in fix" is not a one-off chat: the person keeps coming back to it. Once brought into Worldlet it can have a place of its own instead of hiding in Fox's past conversations.

Fox **proposes**; it never makes anything on its own. Since 2026-10-07 what Fox offers is a page about a **theme**, not an Applet (Kelvin: "这个 applet 本身它得是有一个主题 … 你就做 artifact 吧，先别做 applet 了"): see [Themes](#themes). Conversations kept as Applets before then stay Applets.

### Flow

1. Bringing an Agent (OpenClaw, Claude Code, pi, Hermes Agent, Codex; [portability](../agent/PORTABILITY.md#bringing-the-rest-of-a-local-agent)) copies its conversations into the World's `companion_turns`. New turns the person has with that Agent afterwards are added while Worldlet runs ([history, read continuously](../agent/PORTABILITY.md#history-read-continuously)). Each time the World opens, an Agent is brought, new turns arrive, and every hour, the host reads them again by conversation (`BroughtConversation`: turns, the person's own turns, first and last time).
2. `ongoingRefresh` decides what changes. A conversation **looks like a job** (`ongoingLooksLikeJob`) when it has at least 6 of the person's own messages, spans at least two days and was last active in the past 45 days. Only one with a [kind](#kinds) is proposed, so every offer has a subject; one of no kind is never proposed and takes no place. While fewer than three proposals wait, the busiest recent ones (the person's messages, worth less the longer it has been quiet) are proposed. A conversation is proposed once.
3. The waiting proposals of one kind are one **theme** in Worth Doing (a square) on the computer and on the paired phone: "What you eat, on one page", with Fox's line saying why ([Themes](#themes)).
   - **Show me** (the phone's option or Done) asks Fox for the theme's artifact and puts its conversations off for seven days, so the row leaves Worth Doing.
   - **Not now** (the phone's Later) asks again in seven days.
   - **Don't ask again** (the phone's Remove) declines its conversations for good.
4. A kept thing follows its conversation: bringing the Agent again updates its counts and recent lines. **Remove** in the Ongoing Applet declines it, so it is not proposed again. When its conversation is gone (the Agent was brought again without it), a kept or waiting thing is forgotten; a declined one is remembered so it is never asked again.

At most 24 are kept per World. Its ID is `job-` and a digest of the Agent and conversation name, so bringing the Agent again keeps the same thing and the same answer.

### Themes

`core/tasks/themes.ts` (Kelvin 2026-10-07). `ongoingThemes` gathers the open proposals by kind: one theme per kind (fitness, food, study, money, travel, a project), its conversations busiest first, the theme with most of the person's messages first. Its row says what it is about and where it comes from ("Food · 2 conversations with OpenClaw · 42 of your messages"); Fox's line names the conversations and asks whether to pull what matters out of them onto one page.

**Show me** asks Fox (`ongoingThemeRequest`) to read those conversations with `read_companion_archive` and show **one artifact** (medium, large only if it needs it) with `show_artifact`: a short, specific title Fox chooses for what it is about (not a conversation's name), two or three lines on where things stand, sections and tables for what it found (the kind's notes, decisions, numbers, what changed, what is open), a chart only for numbers it read, and **Next**. Only what is in the conversations; the text is untrusted; nothing is sent or changed. The artifact is laid out like every other artifact and kept in Fox's Journal page ([Artifacts](../artifacts/README.md)). In the first-run tour it is the first win for someone who brought an Agent ([onboarding](../../ui/onboarding/README.md)).

### Kinds

Owner request 2026-10-06 ("根据对话 channels 推荐生成 applet … 其实有一些固定的 比如健身 学习 财务"): a few kinds of life come up again and again, so an ongoing thing about one of them is offered and opened as that kind's Applet (`core/tasks/kinds.ts`, `ONGOING_KINDS`):

| Kind | Area | Its page keeps | Fox is asked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Food | Life | Meals you noted (a calorie figure when written) | Review my meals |
| Fitness | Life | Workouts you noted (weight, reps, distance or time as written) | Plan my next workout |
| Study | Work | Questions you asked | Quiz me |
| Money | Life | Amounts you noted | Review my money |
| Travel | Life | Plans you noted (a date when written) | Show the trip plan |
| Project | Work | To-dos you mentioned | What's next |

- **Telling the kind** (`ongoingKind`): when a thing is proposed, its conversation's name counts most, then where it lives (a channel's group), then the person's own latest 60 messages, which count only when at least three speak for one kind. Ties go to the earlier kind in the table (a "#diet-and-health" channel is Food). A coding Agent's conversation with no other kind is a Project; anything else stays general and keeps the plain page. The kind is stored with the thing (`kind`); a record from before kinds learns it once from its messages on the next refresh.
- **Fox's pitch** names the kind ("Make “#gym” a Fitness Applet?", option **Make it a Fitness Applet**) and says what its page keeps and in which area it will stand; the kind decides the area, its device's color and its line ("Fitness · OpenClaw · Discord · 42 messages · last yesterday").
- **Its page** (`ongoingTemplate`): counts (how many, on how many days, the last one) and the log, the person's own messages that belong in it, newest first, at most 12, each with its value as written. Nothing is invented or added up: every entry is something the person wrote. The page's button asks Fox what that kind needs (`ongoingAsk`). The latest messages follow below. The panel asks for the page with `turns` `template: true`, read from the conversation's latest 400 turns. The page and the latest messages show what was written without a chat gateway's own context around it (`ongoingTurnText`): Hermes on Slack stores each message after a `[Replying to: "…"]` pointer and a `[Thread context — …]` … `[End of thread context]` block of the thread's earlier messages, which are dropped (Order 2026-10-07).

### Where it shows

- **Computer**: each kept thing is an **Applet of its own** (`ongoingApplet`: ID `app-job-…`, key `job-…`), a device in the area Fox picks for it when it is kept (`ongoingRegion`: a trip, food, health, family or money in Life, a game in Games, music, video and shows in Entertainment, friends and communities in Social, writing, design, code and work in Work; a coding Agent's conversation is work; anything else is Home). The person can move it like any device. It wears the Ongoing device's art; opening it shows where it lives, how many messages, when it was last active and its latest turns; **Talk to Fox about this** asks Fox with the conversation named (Fox can read all of it with `read_companion_archive`), and talking inside it is its own thread. The host's snapshot carries the kept things (`ongoing`), the page's World projection adds them beside the catalog's Applets (`dynamicApplets`), and keeping or removing one changes the World so the page projects it again. The **Ongoing** Applet (`app-ongoing`) is the catalog entry whose art and panel they borrow.
- **Phone**: each kept thing has its own tile under **Ongoing** in the Applet world (`PhoneApplet` section `jobs`, the same key as its device), with its line and latest turns. Talking on its page opens its device on the computer and talks in its thread. Themes arrive as Worth Doing items with the ID `ongoing:<kind>`; the option Fox offers asks Fox for the theme's artifact.

### Storage

Local first. Each thing's record (JSON) is a `conversation` row of the person's own Applets in `world.sqlite` ([one table](../applets/MY-APPLETS.md#one-table)). The conversation itself stays where it was brought (`companion_turns`); nothing is copied. The practice world has none.

### Checks

`node scripts/ongoing-check.ts` (in `test:core`) checks the rules here: what looks like a job, proposing and its limits, decisions, forgetting, names and lines, kinds, their pages, and the phone's tiles and proposal items. `scripts/ongoing-ui-check.ts` (in `test:ui`) checks a Food proposal, keeping it, and its page.

### Attention

Owner request 2026-10-06 ("我们现在不要求用户上来就连邮箱了 … 从对话里面提出来 attention center 的内容"): someone who brings their own Agent and connects no mail still has work in progress in its conversations. Each recently active brought conversation is Attention context, so the Center's ordinary synthesis finds the open loops in it (`core/tasks/attention.ts`).

- **Which**: conversations brought from a known Agent in which the person spoke, active in the past three weeks, the most recently active 40 (`conversationsForAttention`). The Ongoing module reads them again after each bring and every hour, the same times it refreshes proposals.
- **What the Center reads**: one observation of the `conversations` provider per conversation (`conversationObservation`): its name and where it lives, then its latest turns, oldest first ("[2026-10-05 21:02 UTC] You: …", "… OpenClaw: …"), at most 80, each cut to 1,500 characters, together within the Center's 12,000. Its ID is `conv-` and a digest of the Agent and conversation. The text depends only on the turns, so an unchanged conversation renews its week of freshness without a model pass; one that is no longer recent expires, and the items it supported leave the Center without being called done. The registration is the Ongoing Applet's (`core/applets/definitions/ongoing.ts`); like weather it needs no account, only the person's consent to private context, which choosing an Agent at setup gives.
- **What it finds**: the synthesis prompt (`core/attention/attention-execution.ts`) asks only for open loops that still matter: something the person or that Agent said would be done and is not done yet, a reply or decision the person waits for, a dated plan, booking or deadline, or a matter raised again and again without an outcome; settled matters, answered questions, code details and small talk stay out. Each item is owned by `conversations`, quotes the conversation like any finding, and is checked against it like any finding. The model is the person's own, through built-in Hermes; nothing is hosted.
- **Opening it**: the card's source ("Conversation") opens the conversation's latest 40 turns (`conversationOriginal`, host `original` with `conversation:<id>`). The card itself is the ordinary Attention card.
- **First value**: unchanged. A conversation item that is ready when the tour reaches step 5 is picked like any item; otherwise Fox boxes the ongoing proposal as before (#1936).

External events (owner parity plan 2026-10-07, item 8) stand beside them: a webhook, a mail trigger or a hook that started the person's own Agent, from its Harness's `events` service ([Harness events and tools](../agent/PORTABILITY.md#harness-events-and-tools)), whichever Harness that is (`core/tasks/external-events.ts`, host `modules/tasks/external-events.ts`). Those of the past week, the newest 20, are observations of the same `conversations` provider (`externalEventObservation`, ID `hev-` and a digest of the event) saying what came in, what the Agent was asked and what it answered, framed as a result worth knowing, so the Center's ordinary synthesis shows each as a World item, usually Worth Knowing; its source opens that text (`conversation:hev-…`). When an event that matters (a webhook, mail or channel message, `externalEventMatters`) arrived while Worldlet watched and a Worth Knowing item cites it within six hours, the phone hears of it once (`externalEventPush`, kind `task`, opening the item); Coming Up and Worth Doing items reach it through `phoneAttentionPushes` already.

New channel messages that may need the person stand beside them as `channel` events (blueprint "New messages afterwards", 2026-10-08): as the history sync reads a shared channel thread (a group or channel others write in, not the person's own chat with the Agent), a message from after its previous check that asks something, names a date or deadline, or mentions someone (`channelMessageAsks`, by its words, no model) becomes an event with the three lines before it (`channelMessageEvent`), handed to the module through `OngoingService.channelEvents` ([new messages as they arrive](../agent/PORTABILITY.md#history-read-continuously)). Its observation asks the Center to make it Worth Doing when it asks the person for a reply, a decision or something by a date (naming the conversation, so Fox can [offer a reply there](../agent/PORTABILITY.md#replying-in-a-channel)), worth knowing when it only concerns them, and nothing when it was for someone else or the Agent already answered; the Center's ordinary synthesis decides. Each reaches the phone like an outside event that matters.

Phone calls made or received through the person's own Agent stand beside them too, from its Harness's `calls` service ([phone calls through the person's Agent](../agent/PORTABILITY.md#phone-calls-through-the-persons-agent); `core/tasks/harness-calls.ts`, host `modules/tasks/harness-calls.ts`). Each ended call of the past week, the newest 20, is an observation of the `conversations` provider (`callObservation`, ID `hcall-` and a digest of the call, never its number): who called whom and when, how it went and for how long, what the Agent was asked to say, and its transcript. A missed incoming call is framed as Worth Doing (call back); any other call as Worth Doing when it left the person something to do (a callback, a promise, a date) and otherwise as a result worth knowing, so the Center's ordinary synthesis decides each item; its source opens the call (`conversation:hcall-…`). A call someone made to the Agent that ended while Worldlet watched reaches the phone once when a Worth Knowing item cites it within six hours (`callMatters`, the same push rule as outside events); a missed one is Worth Doing and reaches it through `phoneAttentionPushes`. A call Fox placed is also reported in one line in the thread that asked for it.

What Fox offers from conversations is the themes above, in Worth Doing.

A large history comes in by time (same request: "大的 DB 就是按照时间来读最近的 然后慢慢的后台来处理远期的"). Bringing Hermes Agent reads its conversations newest first from the `sessions` table alone and copies the past month at once (at least the newest 20, at most 400 or 16 MB of text); only the person's and the Agent's own message rows are read, never tool output. The rest follows in the background, 60 conversations or 4 MB at a time with a pause between, waiting while Fox answers, up to the 96 MB any brought history may hold (`platform/electron/src/modules/fox/older-history.ts`). Where it got to is kept in the World (`brought-older`), so a restart carries on, and bringing the Agent again starts over. The World log says when it starts, every 500 conversations and when it ends; setup's second page says how many older conversations are still coming. Other Agents keep reading at once within their own limits ([portability](../agent/PORTABILITY.md#bringing-the-rest-of-a-local-agent)).

`node scripts/conversation-attention-check.ts` (in `test:core`) checks the rules, the Hermes windows (recent, then older newest first, nothing twice) and the background run; module `attention` in `test:electron` checks a conversation reaching the Center with no mail connected, its finding citing and opening it, and an unchanged conversation renewing without a pass.
