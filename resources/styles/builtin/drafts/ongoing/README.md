# Ongoing device

The [Ongoing](../../../../../ui/applets/ongoing/applet.md) Applet's device is the painted screen stand of the dormant `things` Peek sprite, with its frame repainted a sage green and its face redrawn as a conversation carried on: two speech bubbles over a thread of days with the latest one lit. No image model was used and no third-party artwork was added.

Run `python3 resources/styles/builtin/drafts/ongoing/compose.py` (Pillow and NumPy) to rewrite `assets/applets/ongoing/peek-logo-v3.png`. The output is deterministic.
