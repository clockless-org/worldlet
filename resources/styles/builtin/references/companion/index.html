Research and implementation preserved

Art & anatomy

Keep the accepted painted 2D appearance. Separate body, forelimbs, palm/finger studies, eyes, ear roots, neck and hidden surfaces. Curved length-preserving limb skin and supported body deformation; no exposed head scaling/shear. 3D/Blender and paid Spine were not selected for the current route.

One persistent performance player

Per-entry state clocks, finite phrases, held motion and cancellation. Interrupted gestures must not start new waves/nods. Sparse and 30/60/120 Hz sampling compare timing consistency; this does not prove native display performance.

Props have ownership

One retained book or magnifier episode survives conversation. Search exits return the lens before starting another performance. Reading exits finish the safe page phase, close/place/release the book, then hand over. A parked book remains as an independent lap layer.

Rendering & performance

Shared World/desktop Dev portrait, whole-frame GPU composition, cached eye writes and reused texture allocations. Same-sized pixel updates use texture sub-updates. No resolution or mesh reduction was used to obtain these optimizations.

Observed native limitations

Historical search endpoint check on main 2ef7a8a: RAF median/p95/max 26/36/222 ms; resting 26/38/76 ms. Newer idle and mixed-route samples are recorded below, but are not like-for-like search comparisons or proof of stable 60 fps.

Interaction constraints

One Fox conversation and one dialogue system. Thoughts use head dots, not extra conversation bubbles or fake progress messages. Motion must reflect actual activity, not manufacture background work or success. Keep the deferred twelve disabled.

Known rejected shortcuts: wrist-pivot alignment alone does not align painted palm surfaces; numerical contact alone does not prove correct occlusion. RGB-crossfaded eyelid patches exposed doubled edges; contour/threshold experiments were rejected. Do not resurrect these as accepted solutions.
