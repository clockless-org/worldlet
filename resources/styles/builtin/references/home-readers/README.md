# Home readers

Built-in imagegen, September 28, 2026. `open-focus-v1.png` is a design study,
not shipped record content. `../../assets/applets/google-calendar/open-v2.png`
is the transparent blank calendar plate consumed by the live Open renderer.
Existing Peek devices, positions, World and Fox are unchanged.

Design prompt: four paired Open/Focus views in muted Village paper/forest/brass
materials. Mail shows sender at upper left, portrait stamp upper right, subject
in the center and relative time lower right; Focus is an unfolded letter.
Calendar is a desk flip planner with Day/Week/Month and dated event leaves.
Notes unfolds the yellow-bound notebook. Reminders unfolds the list clipboard.
Keep controls and content live; actions remain with Fox.

Calendar asset prompt: single front-on blank desk flip calendar, landscape cream
paper filling 90%, thin warm timber base, two chunky brass rings and a muted terracotta-red top binding matching peek-logo-v3.png, restrained
miniature painting, no text/numbers/grid/scenery, transparent exterior, clean
flat content area for HTML date cells. Original image preserved at the versioned
asset path; alpha retained.
