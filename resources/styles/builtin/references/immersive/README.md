## Renderer and safe composition

The original clipping combined subject placement at the content seam with cover cropping and a 2.5% zoom. The current `scene-fit` renderer fills the window with the painting at one uniform scale, right-aligned, with no zoom push. Shrinking it to keep the subject clear of the seam left blurred mirrored margins on the left, top and bottom, which the owner reported as a fault (Order 2026-10-07), so no part of the window is blurred or bare any more. When the manually measured primary-subject boundary in `composition.json` would sit under the reader seam, the painting moves right by at most its outer 3%, keeping primary equipment (to 97%) whole. The build verifies the encoded asset hash for every measurement, preventing reuse of stale bounds after artwork changes. Windows of another aspect ratio crop the painting's top and bottom (35/65) or its low-detail left side, never stretch it.

The generation target places essential activity silhouettes at x72–96%, y16–65%, with foreground space below for Fox. Actual rendered screenshots govern acceptance, because generated placement is not guaranteed by prompts. Active native item summaries and Fox messages remain foreground content and may overlay decorative scenery; these cards are not removed to expose artwork. Compact windows at or below 800px retain the existing single-column layout.
