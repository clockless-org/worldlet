# Artifacts & Journal (UI)

How artifacts look and where they are kept: the card Fox shows over the World (`fox-artifact.ts`), the blocks and pictures inside a card (`artifact-blocks.ts`), the fullest version that fits its room (`artifact-fit.ts`), the list of everything Fox made (`companion-artifacts.ts`) and the Journal book that keeps them by day (`journal-book.ts`). The rules are in [core/artifacts](../../core/artifacts/README.md); the one card design every artifact uses is the [card system](CARD-SYSTEM.md). Styles still live in [attention-preview.css](../attention/attention-preview.css) and [states.css](../components/states.css).

See the [feature table](../../docs/UI-CORE-PLATFORM.md#features).
