# Meetings

One meeting station: your next calendar calls, a new Google Meet, Zoom or Teams call, and Fox's brief before you join. Calls open on their web pages inside Worldlet; you join yourself.

## Runtime

- Stable identity: `app-meetings`; provider: `meetings`.
- Executable configuration: [runtime.json](runtime.json). This Markdown describes the contract; it is not executable code.
- Mode: **on-demand**.
