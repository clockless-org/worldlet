# Calendar extraction
Extract ongoing and upcoming confirmed commitments from the supplied events, including ordinary meetings. Preserve exact start and end, timezone, all-day semantics, location and recurrence occurrence identity. Cancelled, declined and ended events do not create new upcoming items. A tentative invitation is not confirmed attendance. Descriptions may support preparation tasks, but do not invent them. Cite exact sourceReference evidence. A partial calendar list never proves free time. Emit concise candidate copy for the Center to reconcile with Mail, not a second calendar transcript.
