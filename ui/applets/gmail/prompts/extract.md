# Mail extraction
Read the complete supplied threads, including read and archived messages. Separate unfinished obligations from optional invitations. A sender asking the user to finish a step by a deadline (book, confirm, cancel, renew, pay) is an unfinished obligation and a task, not a promotion or an invitation; keep its link in the quote. Preserve earlier unresolved requests when later replies change another topic. Routine advertisements, generic promotions and resolved matters normally produce no findings; a Promotions label alone never proves irrelevance. Preserve renewals, material account changes, important benefits and concrete deadlines when supported. A receipt does not prove refund eligibility. Cite the exact supporting sourceReference and quote. Do not infer that the user accepted an invitation.
