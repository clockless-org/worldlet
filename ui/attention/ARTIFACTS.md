# Artifacts & Journal (UI)

How artifacts look and where they are kept: the card Fox shows over the World (`fox-artifact.ts`), the blocks and pictures inside a card (`artifact-blocks.ts`), the fullest version that fits its room (`artifact-fit.ts`), the Journal's timeline of everything Fox produced, day by day (`companion-artifacts.ts`), and the book it opens in (`journal-book.ts`). The rules are in [context/artifacts](../../context/store/ARTIFACTS.md); the one card design every artifact uses is the [card system](CARD-SYSTEM.md). Styles: [artifact-card.css](artifact-card.css) (on the Attention card's parts) and [journal.css](journal.css).

See the [feature table](../../docs/UI-CORE-PLATFORM.md#features).
